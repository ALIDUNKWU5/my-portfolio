FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what clients ask us most.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most landing pages and small business sites take 1–2 weeks, while larger business sites and online stores usually take 3–6 weeks. We confirm the exact timeline during discovery, and it depends on how quickly feedback and content reach us.

How much does a website cost?

Our packages start from ₦150,000 for a Starter site. The final price depends on the number of pages, features, and integrations you need. Send us your requirements and we will give you a clear quote.

How does payment work?

We agree on the full price before starting. Projects are typically split into milestone payments, with a deposit to begin and the balance due before the final handover.

Will I own my website after it is completed?

Yes. Once the project is fully paid, all code, design assets, and custom components belong entirely to you.

Do you help with domain name and hosting?

Yes. We can register your domain, set up reliable hosting, and configure professional email so that your site is live and ready.

What happens after my website goes live?

Every project includes a 30-day post-launch bug-fix guarantee. After that, we offer optional maintenance plans for updates, backups, and security.
